## 3. Custom Calculated Fields to Create in Tableau

Once the dataset is imported, create the following 3 Calculated Fields in Tableau by right-clicking in the Data Pane -> **Create Calculated Field**:

### 1. `Dew Risk Zone`
*(Categorizes matches where wet ball grip loss becomes severe)*
```tableau
IF [dew_spread] <= 6.0 THEN "Severe Dew Risk (Spread <= 6°C)"
ELSEIF [dew_spread] <= 10.0 THEN "Moderate Dew"
ELSE "Dry / Low Dew"
END
```

### 2. `Dew Condensation Factor`
*(Physical proxy showing saturation proximity)*
```tableau
[temperature] - [dew_point]
```

### 3. `Innings Match Context`
```tableau
IF [innings] = 1 THEN "1st Innings (Setting Target)"
ELSE "2nd Innings (Chasing Under Dew)"
END
```

## 4. The 4 Tableau Visual Sheets to Build

Follow these exact steps to build each sheet in Tableau:

### Sheet 1: Geospatial Stadium Dew Risk Map
* **Objective:** Show all IPL venues across India color-coded by dew severity and sized by runs conceded.
* **How to build:**
  1. Drag `latitude` to **Rows** and `longitude` to **Columns**. (Tableau will automatically render the India map).
  2. Drag `venue` to **Detail** on the Marks card.
  3. Drag `Dew Risk Zone` to **Color** (Set Severe Dew = Red `#E53E3E`, Moderate = Orange, Dry = Blue `#3182CE`).
  4. Drag `economy_rate` (set to `Average`) to **Size**.
  5. Add `is_night_match` and `season_year` to **Filters**.
* **Domain Takeaway for Viva:** Coastal venues (Chennai, Mumbai) consistently light up red, proving atmospheric moisture is spatially concentrated.

---

### Sheet 2: The Core Case Study: Chepauk (Chennai) vs. Chinnaswamy (Bengaluru)
* **Objective:** Visually contrast Bengaluru's aerodynamic high-scoring conditions with Chennai's coastal moisture.
* **How to build:**
  1. Drag `venue` to **Filters** and select only:
     - `MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chepauk`
     - `M Chinnaswamy Stadium`
  2. Drag `venue` to **Columns**.
  3. In **Rows**, place:
     - `AVG(relative_humidity)`
     - `AVG(dew_spread)`
     - `AVG(wind_speed)`
     - `AVG(economy_rate)`
  4. Change mark type to **Bar**.
* **Key Numbers to Display on Tooltips (from Akilan's ML Report):**
  - **Chepauk:** Humidity = `72.9%`, Dew Spread = `5.87°C`, Spin Economy = `7.73 RPO`.
  - **Chinnaswamy:** Humidity = `56.1%`, Dew Spread = `11.0°C`, Wind Speed elevated, Pace Economy = `9.37 RPO`.

---

### Sheet 3: 2nd Innings Dew & Ball Grip Degradation Heatmap
* **Objective:** Prove why bowlers leak runs when dew forms late in the evening.
* **How to build:**
  1. Drag `bowler_type` (`Spin` vs `Pace`) to **Rows**.
  2. Drag `Dew Risk Zone` to **Columns**.
  3. Drag `economy_rate` (set to `Average`) to **Color** and **Label**.
  4. Change mark type to **Square / Heatmap**.
  5. Add a filter for `innings = 2` (2nd Innings Chasing).
* **Domain Takeaway for Viva:** Shows that under Severe Dew, pace bowler economy increases by **+0.86 RPO** and spin bowler economy worsens by **+1.14 RPO** due to wet ball grip loss.

---

### Sheet 4: Multi-Season Climate Stability (2008–2026)
* **Objective:** Prove data integrity and sensor reliability over all 18 IPL seasons.
* **How to build:**
  1. Drag `season_year` to **Columns**.
  2. Drag `AVG(temperature)` and `AVG(dew_point)` to **Rows** (use dual axis).
  3. Change mark type to **Line**.
* **Domain Takeaway for Viva:** Demonstrates zero missing values across 1,243 matches and consistent climatological sensor logging across 18 tournament years.

## 5. Assembling the Master Dashboard in Tableau

1. Click **New Dashboard** at the bottom of Tableau.
2. Set size to **Generic Desktop (1366 x 768)**.
3. Drag **Sheet 1 (Geospatial Stadium Map)** to the top half (Width: 100%, Height: 50%).
4. Place **Sheet 2 (Chepauk vs. Chinnaswamy)** on the bottom-left and **Sheet 3 (Dew Grip Loss Heatmap)** on the bottom-right.
5. In the top-right corner of Sheet 1, click the filter icon and select **"Use as Filter"**.
   - *Now, when examiners click on "MA Chidambaram Stadium" on the map, the bottom charts instantly filter to Chennai!*
